# Why does engineering experience not compound?

## Question

Why do commits, reviews, incidents, documents, tests, and session traces so often preserve what happened without making the useful lesson easier to find, verify, and apply next time?

## Working Direction

Experience does not compound when its lesson remains in a record that has no clear owner, boundary, revision path, or next-use context. The artifact survives, but the next actor has to reconstruct the reasoning from scratch or trust an unverified summary.

## Early Test

When a later actor returns to the work, can they locate the current owner, check the source within their available environment, and return a repair when the lesson no longer applies? If not, the experience may be preserved but has not yet become reusable engineering memory.
