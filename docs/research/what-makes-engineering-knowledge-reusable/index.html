Question

What conditions let an engineering lesson be reused without turning it into stale folklore or detached documentation?

Working Criteria

Reusable engineering knowledge should have an owner, a boundary, evidence, a revision path, and a clear next-use context.

Early Test

A lesson is reusable when it can guide a future decision and still point back to the operational truth that could revise or invalidate it.
